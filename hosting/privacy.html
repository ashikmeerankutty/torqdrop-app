TorqDrop

Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

TorqDrop is a car inspection and diagnostics app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the TorqDrop app and to report pages at torqdrop-connect.web.app.

TorqDrop is run by TorqDrop. Contact us at support@torqdrop.com.

What we collect

  • Account: the phone number or email address you sign in with, and the full name and email you give us.
  • Inspections: the vehicle details and answers you enter (such as registration number, make, model and odometer), your remarks, the photos you take, and the name you sign off with.
  • Documents you scan: photos of a registration certificate (RC) or insurance policy, and the details read from them (such as registration and expiry dates, owner count and hypothecation). We do not save owner names or addresses as data, but they may be visible in the document photo you keep.
  • Vehicle diagnostics: trouble codes and readings from a connected OBD adapter, and the adapter's name and protocol.
  • Engine sound clips: when you use the engine sound check, a video of up to 20 seconds with sound, recorded with your camera and microphone, plus any live readings from your OBD adapter during it. The microphone is only used while you record a clip. The clip is deleted from our storage as soon as it has been analysed; the result is kept only on your phone.
  • Symptoms you describe when asking for an explanation of a problem. We remove email addresses, phone numbers and registration numbers from this text before it is analysed.
  • Purchases: when you buy a report pack, Google Play takes the payment. We receive the order ID, the pack and the time of purchase, and keep your report balance. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Usage records: when AI features were used, to apply fair-use limits and track cost.
  • App integrity: a token from Google Play Integrity (Firebase App Check) that proves requests come from the real app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not show ads.

How we use it

  • To run your inspections, store your evidence and produce reports.
  • To provide AI features: checking photos, reading documents, listening to engine sound clips, explaining trouble codes and summarising inspections.
  • To add the reports you buy to your account, and to handle refunds.
  • To keep the service secure and to apply fair-use limits.
  • To contact you about your account.

AI processing

When you use AI features, the relevant photos, document photos, engine sound clips (video and audio), trouble codes, live readings, symptom text and inspection summaries are sent to Meta's model API for analysis. We do not send your name, email or phone number with them. Meta handles this data under its developer terms. AI results are always labelled in the app and must be confirmed by a person before they count in a report.

Who we share it with

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) hosts our database, files and servers. Inspection data, photos and reports are stored in Google's Mumbai region (asia-south1). Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Meta processes data for AI features, as described above.
  • Google Play processes payments for report packs under Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • People you share a report with. A report link lets anyone who has it view that report and its photos, including the registration number, for 14 days.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and inspections until you delete your account. Engine sound clips are deleted as soon as they have been analysed. Shared report links stop working after 14 days. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our database and file storage straight away; copies in Google's routine backups expire on Google's schedule. Order records (order ID, pack and date) are kept, no longer linked to your account, for as long as Indian tax law requires.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to have a grievance addressed. You can:

  • correct your name and email in the app under Account → Your details;
  • delete your account and all its data in the app under Account → Delete account, or as described on our account deletion page;
  • email support@torqdrop.com for anything else, including grievances. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited to your own account by our security rules, photos are never made public, and report links use long random codes that expire.

Children

TorqDrop is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.

TorqDrop is an inspection assistant, not a mechanical guarantee.
