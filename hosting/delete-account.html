Delete your TorqDrop account

In the app

  1. Open TorqDrop and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@torqdrop.com from the email address on your account, or include the phone number you sign in with. We will confirm the request with you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, name and email
  • All inspections, with their answers, photos, AI findings and OBD readings
  • Scanned RC and insurance details and photos
  • Signed-off reports, and any report links you shared (they stop working)
  • Your AI usage records
  • Your report balance and its history. Unused reports are lost and aren't refunded.

Nothing else is kept after deletion, except order records (order ID, pack and date), which we keep without any link to your account for as long as Indian tax law requires, and copies in Google's routine backups, which expire on Google's schedule. Deletion can't be undone.

See also our privacy policy.
